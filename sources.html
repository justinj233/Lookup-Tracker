Sources and licences

Lookup Tracker is free, shows no advertising and is not sold. It combines data from the two projects below.

Words and definitions: JMdict

This app uses the JMdict/EDICT dictionary file. This file is the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group, and is used in conformance with the Group's licence.

JMdict is made available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence. The dictionary file this app downloads (data/dict.txt) is derived from JMdict and is offered under the same licence.

The JSON edition of JMdict used to build it comes from the jmdict-simplified project.

The JMdict version in use and its date are shown on the Counts tab. The file is rebuilt from a newer JMdict from time to time, and the app offers the update when one is published.

Pitch accents: Wadoku

The pitch accent patterns come from Wadoku (www.wadoku.de), the Japanese-German dictionary project started by Ulrich Apel and carried on by him and the volunteers of Wadoku e.V. Copyright in the Wadoku data belongs to Ulrich Apel and the other contributors.

The data is used under the Wadoku data licence, which permits free, non-commercial use with a full statement of its origin:

Wadoku.de-Daten Lizenz (the current data licence)
WaDoku-Datei-Lizenz (Ulrich Apel's original licence)

What this app holds is an extract: for each word, its reading and its high/low pitch pattern. The extract was taken from the list of Wadoku accents distributed with the Japanese Pitch Accent add-on for Anki, and matched to JMdict entries by spelling and reading. Any mismatch between a word and its accent is this app's error, not Wadoku's.

Pitch accent drawings

The drawing code is a port of draw_pitch.py from the Japanese Pitch Accent add-on for Anki.
